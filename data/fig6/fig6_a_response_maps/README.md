# fig6_a_response_maps

Figure 6, panel a. Title: "Tablet (neutral)", "Tablet (social)", "Intruder", "Mask", "Treat", "Toys", "Noise", "Object", "Cage space", "Behavioral space".

What the panel draws, in the data coordinates of each of its subplots: its points, lines, bars, maps and shapes as CSV, photographs and video frames as PNG, and every text printed on it in `annotations.csv`.

- Redrawn from this folder alone by [`code/fig6/fig6_a_response_maps.py`](../../../code/fig6/fig6_a_response_maps.py).

### `annotations.csv` (703 B)

Every text printed in the panel: inside a subplot (x and y in that subplot's data coordinates; for an annotation with a leader line, the point it marks in `target x` and `target y`) or on the page around the subplots (x and y in inches from the page's top-left: the text's anchor).

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `subplot` | the subplot the text is in; blank for page text |
| `text` | the text as printed, on one line |
| `x`, `y` | its position (see `coordinates`) |
| `coordinates` | "data" or "page inches" |
